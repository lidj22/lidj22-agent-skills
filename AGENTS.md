# Contribution guidelines

## Definitions

- Unit of contribution: a PR/issue description, note, comment, or any editable item that cannot be reduced further into units.
- Mechanical update: an update (by an agent) to a unit of contribution which involves no open interpretation of authors intention.

Example of mechanical changes:

- replacing one word with another
    - fixing typo
- deletions
    - deletions of incorrect statements/phrases and repairing grammar

Example of non-mechanical changes:

- any change that introduces prose

## Rules

Applies to all agents working on this project.

- Any unit of contribution (PR, issue, note) authored by an agent must end with coding agent attribution footnote outlining runtime, model ID. Units without said footnote are authored and owned by the human owner.
- Any non-mechanical update to an existing unit of contribution (PR description, issue, note) owned by a human is not permitted. Submit a separate attachment instead under owner approval.
- Any mechanical update to a humans owned unit of contribution may not include a footnote.
