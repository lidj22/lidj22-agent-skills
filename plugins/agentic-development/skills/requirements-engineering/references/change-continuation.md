# Change and continuation

The work product is a recovered or updated requirements record with affected dependencies accounted for.

## Recover the state

Locate the current record, supporting sources, established decisions, and outstanding work. Distinguish an authoritative designation from a proposed location. Missing history leaves acceptance unknown. Identify what the current assignment asks to resume or change and the authorization for editing.

For a continuation without changed evidence, resume the unresolved artifact work using the appropriate route. Recover only the context needed to preserve settled meaning and the requested decision.

## Propagate a change

Identify the premise or requirement changed by new evidence. Use the [content model](content-model.md) when dependencies span artifacts or abstraction layers, and [process foundations](foundations.md) when architecture discoveries require requirements refinement. Inspect dependent requirements, assumptions, scenarios, examples, and readiness claims. Distinguish supersession from a source that merely adds a case or qualifies an earlier statement.

Apply supported corrections within the grant, preserve unaffected requirements, and present additional choices separately. Flag dependencies that are unavailable or cannot yet be resolved so they remain visibly unsettled. Revisit stakeholder validation when the change alters whose need is served or the outcome previously agreed.

Keep the operative record current and change rationale in the appropriate change record. Report the changed meaning, remaining dependencies, and decision now supported without claiming acceptance from the fact that an update is complete.
