# Record discipline

## Sources and authority

Read relevant passages, project rules, and the existing record before asking for context. Retrieve accessible missing evidence. Preserve source qualifications, boundaries, exceptions, and uncertainty when deriving requirements. A source link must retain enough context to recover the intended meaning.

Keep requirements, interpretations, and assumptions distinguishable wherever the difference affects a decision. An example illustrates a possibility unless its source establishes an exhaustive rule. Agent recommendations remain proposals. Authorship and confidence do not establish authority.

Preserve the criterion attached to a delegated decision. Investigate the evidence needed to choose against it; when evidence is unavailable, expose the dependency or propose a provisional choice with its basis. Permission to choose supplies authority, not missing evidence.

## Operative record

Use an established authoritative record where available. Otherwise propose the existing or new artifact to maintain, with its status; selecting a working draft does not establish its authority. Maintain one operative account and keep change history in source control or the appropriate change record. Reusable requirements exclude private session identifiers and incidental conversation detail.

Use the project's vocabulary and artifact structure. Keep material source, interpretation, authority, and disposition relationships inspectable without imposing a schema. Share provenance where clear. State overall draft status once; add local qualifications when their omission would change a decision.

## Open matters and completion

Connect each material open matter to the decision it affects. Separate an investigable fact, a stakeholder choice, and a provisional assumption. Ask now when unresolved alternatives materially affect the current decision and available evidence cannot resolve them; continue unaffected work. An assumption supports a proposal, not an established requirement or authority to act.

A work product is complete when it satisfies the assigned purpose and its material relationships and disposition are inspectable. A requested draft can be complete as a draft. For blocked work, provide the useful partial result, dependency, consequence, and remaining work. Identify the record's location and the next decision it supports.

Acceptance follows the applicable authority, decision process, and existing grants. Review, task completion, and absent history do not establish acceptance. Honor settled decisions and delegated authority without introducing a reconfirmation gate. Design exploration can reveal requirements gaps; keep proposed designs separate from required outcomes.
