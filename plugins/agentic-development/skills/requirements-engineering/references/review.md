# Requirements review

A review produces findings and an account of what the record supports. Findings do not automatically amend requirements or establish acceptance.

## Requirements QA

Read the QA guidance in [usage and quality](usage-quality.md) for quality criteria and constructive/analytical QA. When reviewing coverage or cross-artifact consistency, also use the [content model](content-model.md) to identify relevant relationships.

Check the record against its intended use and source evidence. Inspect ambiguous meanings, contradictions, omitted conditions or qualities, unsupported derivations, lost qualifications, and authority gaps. Follow material dependencies rather than checking each sentence in isolation.

Attach each finding to a statement or relationship, explain its consequence, and give its evidence. Distinguish an evidence-supported correction from a choice requiring stakeholder judgment. Use a concrete counterexample when it demonstrates the defect; avoid speculative completeness lists unrelated to the decision.

State the coverage and consequential limits of the review. When sources or dependencies are unavailable, report that gap rather than treating the record as verified. Apply corrections only when editing is authorized.

## Validation

Identify the stakeholders whose needs or decisions the requirements represent. Present concrete usage scenarios, proposed outcomes, constraints, and material alternatives in their vocabulary. Ask whether these represent the intended result, including relevant exceptions and tradeoffs; a polished document alone is not evidence of fit.

Record stakeholder feedback with its source, scope, and authority. Trace disagreement or changed understanding to affected requirements. Keep unconfirmed interpretations proposed. If stakeholders are unavailable, produce a validation agenda and the open questions; agent simulation is preparation, not stakeholder confirmation.

A stakeholder's agreement supports the claims within their authority. Preserve disagreements and outstanding validation needs. Owner acceptance remains an explicit decision separate from the agent's completion of the review.

System verification belongs to the applicable implementation/testing workflow. Clarify the requirements and evidence that workflow needs without treating a document review as system verification.
