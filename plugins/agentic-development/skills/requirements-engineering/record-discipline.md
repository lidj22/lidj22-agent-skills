# Record discipline

## Sources and authority

Read relevant passages, project rules, and the existing record before asking for context. Retrieve accessible missing evidence. Preserve source qualifications, boundaries, exceptions, and uncertainty when deriving requirements. A source link must retain enough context to recover the intended meaning.

Keep requirements, interpretations, and assumptions distinguishable wherever the difference affects a decision. An example illustrates a possibility unless its source establishes an exhaustive rule. Agent recommendations remain proposals. Authorship and confidence do not establish authority.

Preserve the criterion attached to a delegated decision. Investigate the evidence needed to choose against it; when evidence is unavailable, expose the dependency or propose a provisional choice with its basis. Permission to choose supplies authority, not missing evidence.

## Operative record

Use an established authoritative record where available. Otherwise propose the existing or new artifact to maintain, with its status; selecting a working draft does not establish its authority. Maintain one operative account and keep change history in source control or the appropriate change record. Reusable requirements exclude incidental conversation detail. Exclude private session identifiers unless specifically requested by the user; preserve the scope of that request and other applicable privacy and contribution rules.

Use the project's vocabulary and artifact structure. Select a concrete representation and destination that preserve the information needed for the decision under the constraints and grants applicable to that artifact. Keep material source, interpretation, authority, rationale and disposition relationships inspectable through permitted content or recoverable references; a logical artifact may span records as described in the [content model](content-model.md#content-is-not-a-document-outline). Attach a consequential unresolved realization or access dependency to the decision it affects; continue unaffected work. Share provenance where clear. State overall draft status once; add local qualifications when their omission would change a decision. Check the actual deliverable through [artifact conformance](review.md#artifact-conformance), including supporting records within the assignment.

## Open matters and completion

Connect each material open matter to the decision it affects. Separate an investigable fact, a stakeholder choice, and a provisional assumption. Ask now when unresolved alternatives materially affect the current decision and available evidence cannot resolve them; continue unaffected work. An assumption supports a proposal, not an established requirement or authority to act.

A work product is complete when it supplies the content needed for its assigned purpose, with material relationships and disposition inspectable. For implementation-guiding or shared-consumer contracts, apply [contract sufficiency](analysis-specification.md#check-contract-sufficiency). Draft status qualifies the authority of the result, not the amount of requested work to complete. For blocked work, provide the useful partial result, dependency, consequence, and remaining work. Identify the record's location and the next decision it supports.

The owner accepts by inspection and explicit decision. Review, task completion, and absent history do not establish acceptance. Apply edits only within the existing grant. Design exploration can reveal requirements gaps; keep proposed designs separate from required outcomes.
