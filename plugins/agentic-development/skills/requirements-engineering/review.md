# Requirements review

A review produces findings and an inspectable account of what the record supports. Findings do not automatically amend requirements or establish acceptance.

## Requirements QA

Read the QA guidance in [usage and quality](usage-quality.md) for quality criteria and constructive/analytical QA. Use the [content model](content-model.md) when coverage or consistency spans artifacts.

For proposed requirements or operative guidance, identify the independently actionable rules in the delivered payload and the source obligations they affect. This includes handoffs and summaries that prescribe downstream behavior or claim to preserve source requirements, even when implementation is deferred. Reconcile those rules with the comparative account before claiming review coverage: each material rule or materially interacting group has a comparison, an explicitly bounded unreviewed relationship, or a recoverable operative reference that the consumer must consult. A navigation summary need not reproduce the referenced contract, but must not act as an incompatible substitute. An unreviewed relationship remains unfinished work when required for the assigned result. Include affected source duties omitted from the candidate. Derive coverage from the delivered content rather than topic headings or findings already noticed; unrelated context does not expand the assignment.

Each entry makes the source and candidate locations, applicability, effect, normative strength, and authority or proposed status inspectable. Keep the original source assertion distinct from the interpreted rule. Retain its decision-relevant modal, conditional, and disposition qualifiers before stating the interpretation. Make any changed condition, effect, strength, or status explicit with its source warrant or proposed/uncertain standing; the interpreted account cannot supply its own authority. Interpret ordinary-language force in context rather than assuming a normative-keyword standard the sources have not adopted. Record the established relationship, supported difference, or specific unresolved dimension. Unchanged and unresolved relationships belong in this account as well as defects.

Before giving a compound passage or group a comparison verdict, separate its independently consequential clauses on each side. Carry each clause's controlling scope, definitions, modality, quantifiers, conditions, exceptions and disposition from the surrounding passage; decomposition must not detach a clause from what qualifies it. Establish the material source clauses from the relevant original passages and their governing context, independently of the candidate clause list. Keep literal source evidence distinct from interpretation; a paraphrase may express an interpretation but cannot serve as an exact source anchor. Reconcile that source-derived set with the final-candidate set: map each source clause to the exact final candidate clause or an explicit omission, and each candidate clause to its source basis or proposed departure. Account for unmatched material meaning on either side; reversing candidate-selected links does not establish source coverage. An indexed or cited block is not evidence that all its clauses have been compared. Apply the meaning comparison to those mapped clauses and their material interactions before summarizing the group. A checked clause or representative example does not establish preservation of the remaining clauses. Keep a combined entry only when these clause relationships and their comparison results remain inspectable; no fixed table or rewritten source format is required.

The form may follow the existing artifact; the compared meaning, rather than a preservation label, is the evidence.

An uncertainty is local to the aspect the evidence leaves open. Compare the other established aspects of that rule; an unresolved outcome does not make known applicability unknown. Keep an unreviewed relationship distinct from an unresolved stakeholder choice. Use the comparison below to substantiate the account.

## Compare source and candidate meaning

Express source and candidate applicability separately, resolving operative terms under their applicable definitions. Where a comparison depends on a defined term, substitute its defining conditions into the applicability account and preserve whether the source states a definition, an example, or an open-ended inclusion. Reusing a label does not establish equal scope; keep independently scoped duties distinct even when they share a passage. Inspect cases governed by both rules, only the source, or only the candidate using a small decision table or equivalent representation. Agreement in shared cases does not establish equal applicability.

Anchor each material preservation claim to the exact operative source and candidate wording, with enough context to determine their force; keep paraphrase separate from both. Seek a feasible action or implementation that satisfies the candidate but violates the source, then reverse the comparison to expose added restrictions. Check both differing applicability and differing compliance within shared applicability, including required, permitted, prohibited, or classification results, bounds, exceptions, and discretion. A witness defeats the corresponding preservation claim even when the formulations share a topic, purpose, or owner. Record the consequential case and result in the comparative account. Check uncertain membership and interpretation against the original passages. Where no witness is found, state the comparison basis and remaining limits; search failure does not prove equivalence. Recheck the final operative wording after correction, not only the finding or an intermediate interpretation.

Account for other applicable duties and grants before drawing a final action conclusion. Non-applicability of one rule does not establish permission. A classification difference can remain material when another rule makes the final action the same. A derived account, plausible interpretation, or reviewer agreement does not establish source authority.

For a consequential interpreted change, identify the source assertions that warrant it. Test a claim of necessity against a source-compatible alternative that does not require the change. If such an alternative remains, distinguish the proposed choice from what follows from the source. A conflict-based warrant must show incompatible results under jointly applicable conditions; inconvenience or unreliable factual inference alone does not establish a conflict between operative rules. Check alternatives against the original definitions, scope, authority, and grants. Failure to find an alternative does not prove entailment. Keep unsupported or unresolved interpretive steps explicit rather than letting the derived account certify them.

Distinguish an intentional policy proposal from a faithful restatement. Proposed status permits a choice to be offered; it does not justify selecting it for the recommended result. For a material departure in that result, connect it to the assigned need and explain its advantage and consequences relative to an adequate source-compatible alternative, including effects on legitimate work. If the departure has no task-relevant decision basis and the supported alternative serves the assignment, use that alternative rather than making adoption of the departure an owner decision prerequisite. Where a new choice is needed, give a reasoned proposal or expose the consequential unresolved choice without claiming source authority. Keep its scope, effect, and disposition inspectable.

## Findings and revision

Check the assigned record against its intended use for ambiguity, contradictions, omitted conditions or qualities, unsupported derivations, lost qualifications, and authority gaps. Follow material dependencies across statements and artifacts. Attach each finding to affected content, evidence, and consequence; distinguish a supported correction from a stakeholder choice. A counterexample supports a particular relationship, not universal completeness.

State coverage and consequential limits. Missing sources or dependencies leave the corresponding comparison unverified; continue useful work on the available evidence. Apply corrections only within the editing grant.

## Artifact conformance

Check the actual artifact against the requirements and grants governing its content, representation, destination and use. Include newly created records and supporting material within the assigned scope. When proposed content is intended for another artifact, also check the exact resulting payload in that destination's representation, independently of the proposal's representation. Establishing that instructions preserve a source is separate from these artifact checks. Determine applicability from the actual artifact and governing definitions, including any authorized exceptions. Preserve the information needed by its consumer through compliant content or recoverable references. If parsing or rendering can alter required visible or operative content, materialize the payload in temporary output and use a suitable parser or renderer to inspect the result. The proposal's wrapper is not destination content. Keep the check within the existing grant and state relevant untested destination assumptions. Before taking a static-only fallback, check relevant installed capabilities exposed by the environment, including command-line tools or another supported runtime when the initial package path is unavailable. A failed package probe does not establish environment-wide absence. Use an available suitable tool within the grant; otherwise perform static inspection and report the checked capability and remaining limitation. Recheck changed content and affected continuation material.

## Conformance handoff

For a requested review against requirements, recover or confirm the applicable requirements baseline, its disposition, and satisfaction criteria. Reuse an adequate available baseline without creating another record. Make the requested check scope, source/evidence links, and material requirements gaps available to the implementation/testing workflow. Continue the assigned conformance check through that workflow; a requirements note alone does not complete a requested system review. RE clarifies or maintains the requirements, while the verification methods check system fulfillment.

Route a requirements defect to QA or analysis and a changed requirement through change and continuation. Resolving that dependency stays within the existing grant and does not authorize broader redesign. Keep document quality, stakeholder fit, and system conformance findings distinguishable.

## Validation

Identify the stakeholders whose needs or decisions the requirements represent. Present concrete usage scenarios, proposed outcomes, constraints, and material alternatives in their vocabulary. Ask whether these represent the intended result, including relevant exceptions and tradeoffs; a polished document alone is not evidence of fit.

Record stakeholder feedback with its source, scope, and authority. Trace disagreement or changed understanding to affected requirements. Keep unconfirmed interpretations proposed. If stakeholders are unavailable, produce a validation agenda and the open questions; agent simulation is preparation, not stakeholder confirmation.

A stakeholder's agreement supports the claims within their authority. Preserve disagreements and outstanding validation needs. Owner acceptance remains an explicit decision separate from the agent's completion of the review.
